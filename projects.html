Social Impact

Communeat

Communeat is a community-driven UI/UX concept aimed at reducing surplus food waste by building trust, coordination, and safety in local food sharing. The platform connects food donors, receivers, and volunteers through a simple, transparent flow that ensures excess food reaches the right people at the right time.

The project focuses heavily on user trust and accessibility—using clear status updates, verified profiles, and intuitive interactions to remove hesitation around food sharing. Designed for a Designathon Competition 2025 using Figma, this project challenged me to think beyond aesthetics and deeply consider real-world constraints, social impact, and human behavior, resulting in a solution that blends empathy, functionality, and purpose-driven design.

Education & Immersion

CosmoXQuest

CosmoXQuest is an immersive UI/UX concept designed to make learning about the universe engaging, intuitive, and fun. The project transforms complex astronomical information into an interactive experience where users can explore planets, understand their characteristics, and navigate space effortlessly through thoughtful visual hierarchy and motion design.

Built with a strong user-centered approach, CosmoXQuest focuses on simplifying learning through clear interactions, playful micro-animations, and visually rich storytelling. From ideation to high-fidelity prototyping in Figma, the project challenged me to balance education with immersion—resulting in an experience that feels less like studying and more like a guided journey through space.

Experimental UX

SoundScape

SoundScape is an experimental UI/UX concept that reimagines how people discover and experience music by putting visuals and emotion before playlists and genres. Instead of browsing through lists, users enter immersive visual "sound worlds" that represent different moods and energy levels, allowing them to feel the music before they hear it.

The project explores motion-led navigation, abstract interaction patterns, and visual storytelling to create a deeply engaging, sensory-first experience. Designing SoundScape challenged me to balance creativity with usability—ensuring the interface remains intuitive while pushing the boundaries of traditional music platforms. The concept is built with future scalability in mind, with clear pathways for real-world implementation using audio APIs and interactive web technologies.
